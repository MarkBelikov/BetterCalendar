YOUR RECOMMENDED DEADLINE
The app chooses the date and finish time. Correct its starting estimate or priority if needed. Save recalculates this task and future recommendations; missed targets on other tasks stay visible. Existing calendar blocks must be moved with Suggest time.
Local rules recognize common task words and written durations (for example, “2 hours”). They do not understand every task. Large or vague tasks need smaller steps. A recommended deadline becomes a reminder only after you add its time to your calendar.

  
A TIME TO START
Accept a suggestion or pick another time. This adds a calendar block with a reminder 30 minutes before it starts; your task stays on the permanent list until you complete it. Calendar entries without a duration are treated as 30 minutes. Unrecorded commitments are not known.
